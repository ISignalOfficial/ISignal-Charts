How A Contour Map Shows The Slope Of A Plot

From a field survey to working out how steep the land is

Making the map

Step 1

Survey the land

A land surveyor walks around the plot with a machine that captures coordinates and elevation.

Step 2

Record points at regular intervals

Readings are taken at points chosen at regular intervals across the plot.

Step 3

Upload to design software

The data is uploaded to design software such as AutoCAD.

Step 4

Join points of equal height

The software joins points at the same altitude, creating the contour lines of the area. Here, all the 102 m points form one line.

Reading the slope

Step 5

Draw a line for every 1 m

An interval of 1 m means the software draws a line for every 1 m difference in altitude.

Step 6

Measure the gap between lines

The distance between two lines is used to calculate the gradient. Here, the lines are 4 m apart.

Step 7

Divide interval by distance

The ratio of the 1 m interval to the 4 m distance gives a slope of 1:4, or 25%.

Step 8

Closer lines, steeper slope

The closer the lines are, the steeper the slope of that specific area.
